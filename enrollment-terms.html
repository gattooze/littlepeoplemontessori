Terms for enrolled families

These are the terms you agree to when submitting the "Getting to Know Your Child" form as part of enrollment.

Accuracy of information

The details you share with us, including about your child's health, routines, and family circumstances, are used to help our teachers care for your child thoughtfully from day one. Please make sure everything you provide is true and complete to the best of your knowledge, and let us know as soon as anything changes (a new allergy, a change in routine, updated contact details, and so on).

Fees

Fees are payable as agreed at the time of admission. Once paid, fees are non-refundable and non-transferable, in line with the note on every fee receipt we issue.

Attendance and pickup

We release your child only to you, the other parent/guardian, or someone you've specifically named to us as authorised. Please keep this list current — if someone new will be picking your child up, let us know in advance.

Health and safety information

Information you share about allergies, medical needs, medication, or your pediatrician is kept on file so our team can respond appropriately in a medical situation. We treat this information as confidential and share it only with staff directly involved in caring for your child.

Photos and videos

From time to time, we take photos or videos of children during their day at school. By agreeing to these terms, you're okay with your child occasionally appearing in material we share on our website, Instagram, or other promotional materials. If you'd prefer your child not be included, just let us know in writing and we'll make a note of it.

Questions

If anything here doesn't make sense, or you'd like to discuss any of it, please reach out — call or WhatsApp us on +91 91378 80091, or email info@littlepeoplemontessori.com.
